## Confirm before electrical implementation

- Photographs establish visible geometry, not hidden connectivity. Confirm whether U–X, V–Y, W–Z are independent winding pairs and how the marked voltage is specified; do not assume a neutral or internally connected star point.
- Confirm diode types, polarity where unclear, capacitor values/polarities, resistor values where faded, and all internal links from a schematic or user-verified mapping.
- Confirm scope reference/ground connections and the BNC adapter wiring before modeling their effect on the circuit.
- The red multimeters are partly obscured. Do not infer their exact model or dial layout from these photographs.
- Future scope waveforms must be generated by the wired circuit's solver. These photos do not authorize placeholder waveforms presented as simulated results.

No photographed trainer or scope has been implemented merely by adding these references.
