# Load switch (Lastbrytare), Esselte Studium

From [references/machine-lab/load-switch.png](../../references/machine-lab/load-switch.png).

## What the photo shows (confirmed)

- A steel box with a stainless front plate and blue sides. The plate reads **"LASTBRYTARE / LOAD
  SWITCH 380V ~ 16A"** ("lastbrytare" is Swedish for load-break switch).
- A rotary cam switch with a black handle in a square black plate with a cream frame. The marks
  **I** and **0** are legible.
- **Six black knurled 4 mm binding posts**: three in a column on the left, and three on the right
  starting lower, level with the left column's third post (the same stepped layout as the M-G
  set's panel).
- A yellow sticker to the right of the switch. Two leads are plugged into the left middle post and
  the right middle post.

## Model (in the app as `load-switch-v1`)

- Three poles switched together: 1–2, 3–4 and 5–6. The left column (1, 3, 5) faces the right
  column (2, 4, 6). The **numbering is the usual switch convention**, because the plate's own pole
  marks are not legible (**?**).
- Click the handle to turn it to **0** (open) or **I** (closed). A closed pole is 2 mΩ and an open
  pole is 1 GΩ (an open contact, so a voltmeter across it reads the real voltage).
- Sheet 5 uses one pole as the "Switch" between the generator and the load ammeter and load.
